Awards for Women in AI and Cybersecurity, 2026

By Jax Scott, former Special Operations cyber warrant officer and founder of TheZaraAI

The short version

  • Cybersecurity: SC Media's Women in IT Security, the Most Inspiring Women in Cyber Awards, and the Cybersecurity Leader category of the Women in Tech and AI Awards.
  • AI: the Women in AI (WAI) Awards in North America and Asia Pacific, and the VentureBeat Women in AI Awards.
  • Scholarships: WiCyS conference scholarships and ESET's Women in Cybersecurity Scholarship.
  • Most cycles below have closed for this year. Bookmark the official pages and write the nomination now, while the work is fresh.

People search for "AI awards for women" and "top women in AI" because recognition opens doors: speaking slots, board seats, budget. I would rather you nominate someone than wait to be noticed. Here is where to do it.

I only included programs I could confirm on the organizer's own site or announcement. Dates appear only where that official page states them. Programs change every year, so check the official page before you write.

Women in cybersecurity awards

SC Media Women in IT Security

Run by CyberRisk Alliance and its news brand SC Media. The 2025 edition was the program's 12th year, with honorees in four categories: Cybersecurity Veterans, Power Players, Advocates and Women to Watch, chosen by a panel of judges, according to the 2025 honorees announcement. Program news lives on the SC Media Women in IT Security page.

Most Inspiring Women in Cyber Awards

A UK program run through IT Security Guru. Nominations for the 2026 awards opened in November 2025, and the physical ceremony took place on February 26, 2026, per the official nominations announcement. Watch the same site for the next cycle.

Women in Tech and AI Awards: Cybersecurity Leader of the Year

WomenTech Network runs a global, virtual awards program with a Cybersecurity Leader of the Year category and a Responsible AI Leader of the Year category. Its official awards page lists a virtual ceremony on December 3, 2026. A participation fee applies per category, so read the terms first.

Women in AI awards

Women in AI (WAI) Awards North America

Run by Women in AI for women working in AI in Canada, the US and Mexico. Self-nominations are accepted. The official page promotes the next edition in Toronto in June 2027 and has an expression of interest form. The category list includes Trailblazer, Executive Leader, Rising Star and the AI for Good Award (Responsible AI Leader of the Year).

Women in AI (WAI) Awards Asia Pacific

The same organization's APAC awards have a category called AI in Cybersecurity (Risk Management) and another called AI in Defence and Intelligence. The 2026 APAC page lists applications as closed on June 21, 2026, with the awards night on September 3, 2026, in Sydney.

VentureBeat Women in AI Awards

The public submits nominees and VentureBeat picks winners in AI Entrepreneur, AI Research, AI Mentorship, Responsibility and Ethics of AI, and Rising Star. The seventh annual winners were announced at VB Transform in San Francisco in June 2025. Watch VentureBeat for the next call.

Scholarships worth nominating for

WiCyS conference scholarships

Women in CyberSecurity (WiCyS) offers scholarships to its annual conference, covering registration, meals and two nights of shared lodging, less a $75 processing fee. Per the WiCyS 2027 scholarships page, applications for the March 17 to 19, 2027 conference in Denver closed on September 30, 2026.

ESET Women in Cybersecurity Scholarship

For US citizens or permanent residents studying STEM or a cybersecurity field at an accredited college. The official page says the 2026 season has wrapped up; that cycle's deadline was April 8, 2026.

How to write a nomination judges remember

  • Lead with outcomes. "Cut phishing losses" beats "passionate about security." Use numbers the nominee can stand behind.
  • Show who she lifted. Most of these programs ask about mentoring and community. Name the people and programs.
  • Match the category. Read the criteria and answer them in order. Judges score against the rubric, not the vibe.
  • Get her permission and her facts. A nomination with a wrong title or date undercuts everything else.
  • Nominate yourself if the rules allow it. Several do. Waiting to be noticed is not a strategy.

My take: Recognition is not vanity in this field. It is how the next person finds out someone like her does this work. If you know a woman doing serious AI security work, nominate her this cycle.
