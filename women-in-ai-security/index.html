A woman leader in AI security

Why it matters

Who builds AI is a security question.

When everyone designing a system shares the same blind spots, you do not get better AI. You get brittle AI. Attackers look for the case nobody in the room thought of. A wider room finds more of those cases first.

That is why I teach AI to people who were told it is not for them, and why I show women in cybersecurity how to build agents, not just use them. I’m not ahead of you, waiting to pull the ladder up. I’m here to hand it down.
