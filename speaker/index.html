Long bio209 words

Jaclyn “Jax” Scott is an AI security keynote speaker, a former Special Operations cybersecurity officer and the founder of TheZaraAI. She has spent more than 20 years securing systems where failure is not an option.

As a U.S. Army Cyber and Electronic Warfare Special Operations Warrant Officer, Jax supported SOCOM, SOCEUR, the Department of State and NATO. She still serves as a Chief Warrant Officer in the 75th Innovation Command. In the enterprise she was Deputy CISO and VP of Cybersecurity at Pearson.

Jax is co-author of Cybersecurity Career Master Plan and co-host of the award-winning 2 Cyber Chicks podcast. She is an RSA Conference speaker, and as a board member of the Special Operations Association of America she wrote the Jax Act. She holds a master’s degree in Cybersecurity Risk Management from Georgetown University.

Today Jax leads TheZaraAI (thezaraai.com), which helps organizations put AI to work safely through AI operations, secure AI assessments and custom AI agents. Her keynotes turn agentic AI security, Shadow AI, prompt injection, deepfakes and AI governance into decisions that boards and security teams can act on. As a woman in AI security and a female veteran, she also speaks to women in AI and cybersecurity about getting to the front of this wave.
