About Jax

The operator’s view of AI, minus the jargon theater.

I have spent more than 20 years securing systems where failure is not an option. As a U.S. Army Cyber and Electronic Warfare Special Operations Warrant Officer I supported SOCOM, SOCEUR, the Department of State and NATO. I still serve as a Chief Warrant Officer in the 75th Innovation Command, putting AI in the hands of the warfighter.

In the enterprise I was Deputy CISO and VP of Cybersecurity at Pearson. I co-authored Cybersecurity Career Master Plan, co-host the award-winning 2 Cyber Chicks podcast, speak at RSA Conference, and wrote the Jax Act as a board member of the Special Operations Association of America.

Today I run TheZaraAI, where we help organizations adopt AI without losing sight of it. The risk is not using AI poorly. It is running it without visibility.
